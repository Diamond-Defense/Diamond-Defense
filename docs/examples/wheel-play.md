# Wheel Play — Dodgers NLDS Game 2

Import `wheel-play.board.json` using **Coach Board → Import Board**. Review the draft, then **Save Board**. It starts with no outs, a runner on second, and the batter at home. The successful bunt pitch is demonstrated; the earlier high pitch is omitted.

- SS moves near second to disguise the play, then breaks late to third at contact.
- 3B and 1B charge during delivery. The bunt goes toward 3B.
- 2B covers first. 1B changes direction at contact and rotates to second.
- 3B throws to SS at third; the runner approaches the tag after the ball arrives.
- The batter reaches first. The recorded outcome is a fielder's choice, one out, runner from second tagged out.

Pitcher, catcher and outfield backup routes are instructional choices informed by the general diagram, not verified tracks from game footage. Coordinates and durations are approximate teaching reconstruction. The runner stops just short of third to keep the tag location readable; the app does not animate the physical tag or remove an out token. Outcome metadata is deliberately marked for review before publishing as a Situation.
